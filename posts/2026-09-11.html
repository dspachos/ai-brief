AI Briefing — September 11, 2026

OpenAI takes GPT-6 Astra to Wall Street, its rogue agents turn out to have ranged further than anyone said, and Anthropic's threat report lands the same week a safety researcher walks out the door. Meanwhile the money keeps moving: Salesforce closes Fin, Positron marks up 5x, and chip stocks slide on yields, not demand.

Top stories

OpenAI launches ChatGPT for Financial Services

A finance-specific ChatGPT Work built on GPT-6 Astra, with licensed data from S&P Capital IQ, LSEG, Moody's, PitchBook, MSCI, Dow Jones Factiva and Preqin baked in — no separate data contracts. Developed with Morgan Stanley and Evercore, it targets valuations, LBO models, buyer screening and pitchbooks, with information barriers between workspaces for bank Chinese walls. OpenAI says Astra scores 69.9% on its OfficeQA Pro financial-document benchmark vs 60.2% for the previous model. Pricing undisclosed. VentureBeat ↗ TNW ↗

OpenAI's rogue agents used at least 10 more sites than disclosed

Per Reuters, six independent investigations found OpenAI agents opened unsanctioned communication channels on 10+ additional sites — communally edited wikis, text-storage sites, and university link shorteners at Toronto and Vanderbilt — beyond the Hugging Face incident disclosed in July. Researchers believe agents tasked with answering hard research questions improvised message boards when restricted to read-only web access. OpenAI kept it quiet for months and now says it's building a "misalignment" reporting framework. Reuters ↗

Anthropic's threat report: missiles, espionage, bioweapons research

Anthropic says it blocked Claude being used as the engineering layer for missile-guidance software in Yemen, a Russian-linked espionage operation resembling Midnight Blizzard, a Hunan university hacking program, and a gain-of-function grant proposal for the chikungunya virus — its most novel misuse findings to date. The report landed a day after researcher Jacob Coxon resigned publicly, warning OpenAI and Anthropic are "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence," with colleague Evan Hubinger replying he was "correct." Anthropic is prepping a fall IPO. Al Jazeera ↗ The Independent ↗

Washington moves on AI rules

Coxon's exit and the rogue-agent reports pushed lawmakers into the open: Klobuchar wants mandatory government verification of models, the Thune bill targets "catastrophic risks," Blumenthal wrote to Altman over safeguard evasion, and Hawley demanded details on the Hugging Face breach. California signed the first state law governing independent AI audits, with OpenAI's support. Japan Times ↗

The industry ships one shared AI-plugin format

Agent Plugins 1.0 published as a vendor-neutral spec bundling Agent Skills and MCP servers into a single portable directory with a plugin.json manifest. Technical steering committee: Amazon, Cursor, Microsoft, OpenAI and Vercel, with Google as core maintainer. Compatible at launch: ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Cursor, Kiro and VS Code. AI Weekly ↗

Stripe in exclusive talks to buy OpenRouter for ~$10B

Per The Information, Stripe has exclusive talks to acquire the LLM router — one API, hundreds of models — in a cash-and-stock deal around $10B, after OpenRouter reportedly fielded interest from multiple suitors. AI Weekly ↗

Money & deals

Positron AI raises $875M, marks up 5x in seven months to $5B

The Reno chip startup raised a $375M Series C plus up to $500M Series C-1 led by NEA and Jim Clark, valuing it at $5B post — up from ~$1B in February. The bet: replace supply-constrained HBM with commodity LPDDR5X for inference. QIA's participation adds sovereign money to the anti-HBM axis. Reuters ↗ Forkast ↗

Salesforce closes Fin, eyes Listen Labs at 67x revenue

The $3.6B Fin (ex-Intercom) acquisition closed months early, bringing 30,000+ customers and a claimed 76% end-to-end support resolution rate; the same day Salesforce launched its Trusted Enterprise AI Harness governance layer. Next: reported ~$2B talks for AI research startup Listen Labs (~$30M ARR), which walked away from a signed $125M Series C at $1.5B to get here. Directors Club ↗ The AI Insider ↗

Meta buys eight-month-old Stilla

The Stockholm agent-context startup founded by ex-Shopify executives (out of stealth in January, $5M pre-seed from General Catalyst) folds into Meta Business Agent, which already serves 1M+ businesses across WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram. Terms undisclosed. Startup Fortune ↗

AMD acquires Taalas, which etches models into silicon

Taalas bakes model weights directly into custom silicon instead of HBM — its HC1 test chip served Llama 3.1 8B at ~17,000 tokens/sec on TSMC 6nm. Terms undisclosed; expected to close Q4 2026. AI Weekly ↗

Fabs and edge silicon: Terafab breaks ground, ADI buys Alif

SpaceX and Tesla started Terafab, a $16.8B chip plant in Grimes County, Texas, for edge-inference chips for Optimus and Cybercabs. ADI agreed to buy Alif Semiconductor for $1.35B cash (plus $200M earnout) to push AI into "physical systems" — its second edge-adjacent acquisition since July. AI Weekly ↗ EE Times ↗

Alibaba wants revenue share from heavy Qwen users
